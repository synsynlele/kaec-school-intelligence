# KSI Learning amendment

Version 1.2 of the KSI Product Constitution. Approved by the Product Custodian on 21 November 2026.

This amendment supersedes the portal exclusions in section 3 and the student surface retirement in the KSI 2.2 amendment for KSI Learning. KSI retains its three intelligence engines.

- Restore learner access for teacher-released HQLS activities, CBT and published results. Preserve topic suspense until Full Illumination.
- Show the objective score, learner responses and correct answers immediately after final CBT submission. Hold written and critical-thinking marks for human marking and official results for authorised publication.
- Provide a separate parent account and dashboard. Verified parents may view and download each linked child's published diagnosis, midterm report and term report individually or as a single PDF for the term. Preserve human review and keep internal diagnosis evidence private.
- KNS uses 0–49 Incomplete, 50–99 Progressing and 100 Complete. Other schools may configure their own grading policies. Preserve the policy used for published reports.
- Keep school and learner access scoped to verified identities. Reuse assessment marks for reports and diagnosis. Delegate release and publication to authorised school staff.

The first implementation slice is class CBT assignment, learner submission and immediate objective feedback. Written marking, official publication, learner activities and family reporting require further implementation.
