# KAEC School Intelligence — Product Constitution

**Version 1.1 — APPROVED Stage 0 Baseline**  
**Approved:** 7 August 2026

This repository control copy governs implementation of KAEC School Intelligence (KSI) Version 1. It is subordinate to the three HQLS constitutional frameworks where their domains apply and must be read together with `AGENTS.md` and the active stage specification.

## 1. Constitutional authority

The approved HQLS constitutional corpus is:

1. **HQLS Learning & Operations Framework** — final authority on learning, classroom practice, assessment, discipline, teacher behaviour and student experience.
2. **HQLS School Management Framework** — constitutional authority for leadership, governance, people, parent engagement, accountability and continuity.
3. **HQLS Business & Impact Framework** — constitutional authority for money, impact, growth, licensing, replication and business non-negotiables.
4. **HQLS Execution Map** — routes questions to the correct governing framework and prevents personality-driven reinterpretation.
5. Supporting HQLS rubric, induction, prompts, assessment/diagnosis prototypes and parent diagnosis template remain subordinate implementation references.

For cross-domain decisions, use this priority order:

**Human dignity → Learning integrity → System sustainability → Growth and expansion.**

No product metric, customer request, revenue opportunity or implementation shortcut may invert that order.

## 2. Product identity

KAEC School Intelligence is an AI-powered academic intelligence platform that helps teachers design HQLS learning experiences, create aligned assessments, and turn student evidence into actionable diagnosis.

The Version 1 loop is:

**HQLS Lesson → Assessment → Student Evidence → Diagnosis → Action / Intervention → Next HQLS Lesson**

KSI is not a school ERP, generic teacher chatbot, repository of disconnected prompt forms, autonomous replacement for teachers, or clinical/psychological diagnostic system.

## 3. Version 1 boundary

Version 1 contains exactly three core intelligence engines:

1. **HQLS Lesson Intelligence**
2. **Assessment Intelligence**
3. **Student Diagnosis Intelligence**

Permitted supporting capabilities are secure accounts, teacher/school workspace, class/subject context, saved artifacts, private curriculum/reference resources, editing/versioning/history, export, administrative prompt/version controls, quality feedback, and the handoff between the three engines.

Explicitly excluded from Version 1: fees/accounting, attendance, payroll/HR, admissions, transport, timetabling, parent portal/messaging, student portal, full classroom-observation product, teacher certification product, daily/weekly/termly operations modules, PipuPath integration, and general-purpose school leadership dashboards.

A feature is not admitted merely because KAEC owns the idea. It must materially improve HQLS Lesson, Assessment or Diagnosis.

## 4. HQLS lesson law

KSI adopts the seven-stage HQLS learning sequence as authoritative:

1. **Awakening** — curiosity, meaning and identity. No definitions, notes or full teaching.
2. **Exploration** — crude thinking, assumptions and learner voice. Wrong/incomplete thinking may surface without premature correction.
3. **Micro-Illumination** — minimal clarity and guardrails. Enough to prevent hopelessness, not enough to remove struggle.
4. **Trial — First Attempt** — productive struggle and gap exposure. The teacher does not rescue.
5. **Full Illumination** — targeted, concise teaching connected directly to the first attempt and revealed misconceptions.
6. **Trial — Second Attempt** — re-application with improved reasoning and returned learner ownership.
7. **Integration** — reflection, identity and transfer beyond the immediate lesson.

Non-negotiables:

- No full teaching before first meaningful struggle.
- No struggle without guardrails.
- No second attempt without illumination.
- No learning without reflection.
- Student thinking must be visible.
- Teacher restraint must protect learner cognitive ownership.
- Dignity is never sacrificed for order, speed, compliance or performance.

There is no **HQLS Lite** product tier. Commercial tiers may differ in volume, storage, collaboration, reporting or branding; they may not remove sequence fidelity, dignity, responsibility transfer, struggle-before-clarity or reflection.

## 5. Assessment constitution

Assessment exists to reveal understanding, guide growth, inform instruction and build responsibility. It must not rank worth, reward memorisation, create fear or replace teaching.

KSI must support both:

- **Academic Mastery** — quizzes, tests, examinations, written tasks, curriculum knowledge and skill.
- **Human Capability Evidence** — reasoning, explanation, problem-solving approach, persistence, collaboration, reflection quality and responsibility for learning where evidence is available.

Initial assessment item modes:

- Objective
- Subjective
- Critical Thinking
- Project

Initial KAEC Critical Thinking experience types:

- Reality Simulation
- Imperfect Choice
- Hidden Problem
- Creation
- Crisis

Each assessment should preserve topic/objective/type/difficulty/marks/expected-evidence metadata where applicable. If created from an HQLS lesson, the source lesson relationship must be persisted.

## 6. Diagnosis constitution

Diagnosis turns evidence into a clear next action for school, learner and parent. It is educational/student-development diagnosis, not medical, psychiatric or psychological diagnosis.

The reasoning hierarchy is:

**Observed Evidence → Detected Pattern → Possible Interpretation → Recommended Action**

When evidence is inadequate, KSI must state **Insufficient Evidence** rather than manufacture certainty.

Version 1 modes:

1. Quick Teacher Diagnosis
2. Assessment-Based Diagnosis
3. Combined Diagnosis

The parent-facing diagnosis must preserve the KAEC structure:

- student details
- Academics / Skills strengths and challenges
- Character (Discipline) strengths and challenges
- concise Diagnosis
- Action Plan (Academics / Skills): School and Parents
- Action Plan (Character): School and Parents
- Builder Growth Direction
- Encouragement Note
- prepared/approved information and date

A parent-facing diagnosis remains **Draft** until an authorised human review is recorded. Version 1 exports the reviewed report; it does not automatically send it to parents.

## 7. Data and AI architecture rules

- Preserve Lesson → Assessment → Evidence → Diagnosis traceability.
- Store important artifacts as structured data, not only generated prose.
- Separate generation from validation.
- A model declaring its own output valid is insufficient.
- Version AI engines/prompts and persist provider/model/version provenance.
- Keep AI/API secrets server-side.
- Isolate private school resources and student data by workspace.
- Preserve source provenance when uploaded curriculum/resources materially guide an output.
- Provider/model changes must pass the same constitutional evaluation suite.
- Human responsibility remains intact; AI supports judgement rather than replacing it.

## 8. Scale and impact rules

KSI is an authorised non-physical scale vehicle for HQLS only while fidelity can be protected. Scale without fidelity is damage.

Product impact should eventually be interpreted through four HQLS domains where evidence exists:

1. Learning & Thinking Quality
2. Human Formation
3. System Fidelity
4. Sustainability & Health

Usage and revenue are not substitutes for impact. If engagement or commercial metrics rise while HQLS fidelity, dignity or learning quality falls, leadership must treat the apparent win as a warning.

Data may be used to refine practice, adjust training, strengthen systems and challenge assumptions. It must not be used to shame staff or students, rank human worth, or justify predetermined conclusions.

## 9. Release gates

No feature is complete merely because the UI renders or an AI model returns text.

### HQLS gate
- all seven stages in order
- struggle before full explanation
- meaningful learner voice in Exploration
- minimal Micro-Illumination
- genuine first struggle without rescue
- Full Illumination responds to revealed gaps
- genuine second attempt
- reflection present
- teacher remains Guide

### Assessment gate
- requested counts/types correct
- coverage aligned to selected topics/objectives/resources
- answer keys/marking guides internally consistent
- language/difficulty appropriate
- critical-thinking tasks require actual reasoning/choice/creation
- metadata and source links persist

### Diagnosis gate
- evidence is not confused with inference
- unsupported causes are not stated as facts
- school/parent actions are specific and feasible
- parent wording is respectful and readable
- uncertainty is explicit
- final report requires human review
- exported report matches reviewed content

### Platform gate
- no dead-end core flows
- Lesson → Assessment → Diagnosis context transfer works
- saved work survives refresh/re-login
- permissions prevent cross-workspace leakage
- secrets remain server-side
- output schemas validate before persistence
- desktop/mobile remain usable

## 10. Change control

No founder, administrator, engineer, AI model or customer is above the system. Constitutional behaviour may not be silently modified.

Implementation details may evolve without a constitutional amendment only when observable constitutional behaviour remains intact.

Any change to product purpose, HQLS stage behaviour, assessment philosophy, diagnosis standards, parent-report obligations, data-trust rules or Version 1 scope requires an explicit Constitution version increment and KAEC approval.

**Stage 0 is approved and frozen.**
