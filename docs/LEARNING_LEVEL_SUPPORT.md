# KSI learning-level guidance

Owner requested support from nursery through university on 12 October 2026.

One shared `LEARNING_LEVEL_v1.0` resolver/guidance module feeds HQLS generation,
lesson context used in content review and stage repair, both assessment engines,
assessment repairs, and diagnosis generation/repair. Diagnosis now reads the
existing class age range under the same school scope. Next-lesson intervention
handoffs use the existing HQLS engine and therefore receive the same guidance.

Supported guidance profiles: early years, primary, secondary and tertiary.
Use explicit setup labels such as Nursery 1, Primary 4, JSS1, SS2, University
Year 1 or 100 Level, and an accurate age range. Ambiguous labels such as College
or Year 1 without sufficient context are not automatically called university
or primary. Age alone never establishes university enrolment.

Profiles adapt learner language, materials, task load, evidence modes, teaching
depth and adult-appropriate support. The seven HQLS stages, teamwork, suspense,
subject review, material-count checks, uncertainty, human approval and school
boundaries remain. Early-years assessments retain existing schema/count rules
but may be teacher-read, concrete, oral or observational. No new curriculum,
university research sources, learner records or consent is invented.

No new AI call or separate intelligence engine is introduced. No migration,
role permission change, school provisioning or production-data seed is required.
Historical generated artifacts are not rewritten.

Local checks verify representative class-label resolution, ambiguity handling,
shared prompt routing across the three engines and existing constitutional,
quality and export regressions. They do not prove fresh provider output quality
or classroom suitability. Before claiming universal readiness, authorised level
specialists should review representative generated samples and classroom use.
University use still requires actual programme objectives and authorised source
material; this change does not supply a university curriculum catalogue.
